Featured Project · Published Research

Parchify — Intelligent Invoice Processing Platform

AI-powered invoice digitization platform designed to extract structured information from printed and handwritten invoices without relying on fixed templates.

  • Integrated Google Document AI and OCR for invoice data extraction.
  • Added image preprocessing including deskewing, cropping and noise reduction to improve processing of lower-quality scans.
  • Designed a confidence-based validation layer that flags low-confidence fields for human review instead of accepting uncertain data automatically.
  • Built the frontend using React.js and Tailwind CSS.
  • Developed a Python/FastAPI backend with SQLite-based data storage.
  • Added export support for Excel, PDF and JSON.
  • Co-authored research based on the framework, published in the International Journal of Computer Applications.

  • Built a vendor-agnostic invoice processing pipeline (Google Document AI OCR → canonical schema → arithmetic/tax validation) achieving 94.3% OCR accuracy and 91.2% field extraction F1-score, with 88.5% of invoices passing automated validation reducing manual review to 11.5% of volume. Published, IJCA 2026.
